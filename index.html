What happens if your vehicle is a total loss?

If your vehicle was declared a Total Loss by your Motor Insurer, they would generally value the vehicle at its Market Value at the time of the loss. This is likely to be less than what you originally paid for the vehicle.

How APEX could help

APEX is designed to cover the difference between your Motor Insurer's valuation and the amount you originally paid for the vehicle, helping bridge the potential Financial Shortfall.
