01 — What happens if your vehicle is a Total Loss?

If your vehicle was declared a Total Loss by your Motor Insurer, they would normally value the vehicle at its Market Value at the time of the loss.

02 — The Motor Insurer pays the Market Value

This value is likely to be less than the amount you originally paid for the vehicle, potentially leaving you with a Financial Shortfall.

03 — How APEX could help

APEX is designed to cover the difference between your Motor Insurer's valuation and what you originally paid for the vehicle, helping bridge the Financial Shortfall following a Total Loss.
