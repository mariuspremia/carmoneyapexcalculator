3 How APEX could help

APEX is designed to bridge the gap

APEX is designed to cover the difference between your Motor Insurer's valuation and what you originally paid for the vehicle, helping to protect against a potential financial shortfall following a Total Loss, subject to the terms and conditions of the policy.
